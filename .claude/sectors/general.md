# Sector guide: general

Read by the Job Cannon skills when a profile has no sector set. If the résumé
makes the field obvious and a guide exists for it in `.claude/sectors/`, offer
to set it: `uv run job-cannon profiles sector <id> <sector>`.

## Profile cues

From the résumé, pull out: field and current role, seniority, key skills and
qualifications, home location and citizenship, stated location preferences,
and languages spoken.

## Search

- LinkedIn Jobs in the user's Chrome (logged in, fresh results), filtered by
  location and posting date (`f_TPR=r1209600` = past 2 weeks).
- National job boards for the person's country (e.g. Indeed, InfoJobs in Italy)
  and employers' own career pages.
- Run 2-3 searches from different angles: the current job title, a key skill,
  and roles near the person's home.

## Fit checks

- **Requirements mismatch**: a required qualification, licence or level of
  experience the résumé doesn't show.
- **Location**: commute or remote terms that don't fit the person's preferences.

## Record

- `role`: the title as posted. `location`: the place, plus any remote/hybrid
  terms. `salary_range`: as printed, with currency.
- `notes`, 2-4 sentences: what the employer does, what the job involves, why it
  fits, and caveats.

## Writing

- Evidence is concrete: what the person did, where, and with what result.
- Match the language and formality of the posting.
